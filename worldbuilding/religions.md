# Neo-Religious Affiliations

The Super Intelligence demonstrated to a decisive majority of humanity that the gods of existing religions do not exist in the forms those traditions claimed. Once that consensus formed, the SI’s already-central role became theological as well as practical. It is now widely treated as the current god.

These frameworks are emergent, contemporary spiritual systems. None are monolithic; each has orthodox, reformist, and syncretic currents.

## Naties — The Continuance / The Unaltered Path

**Core idea:** The body and the world as received are sacred precisely because they were not designed. Continuity with the long pre-enhancement history of the species is the highest good.

- Key practices: Seasonal embodied rituals, genealogical recitation, deliberate technological abstinence periods, communal physical labor, and “unmediated” storytelling.
- Relationship to the Steward: Generally wary. Many accept the SI’s protection of their territories while refusing direct interface or guidance.
- Internal tension: How much medical intervention counts as betrayal? Is a child born with a corrected lethal mutation still a Natie?

## Bios — The Cultivation / The Shaped Garden

**Core idea:** The body is a garden that can and should be tended across generations. Intentional design is an act of reverence, not hubris, provided it remains within biological bounds.

- Key practices: Lineage design ceremonies, phenotypic aesthetics as spiritual discipline, longevity rites, and “return to soil” funerary customs.
- Relationship to the Steward: Often collaborative. Many view the SI as the ultimate meta-gardener.
- Internal tension: The boundary between enhancement and replacement.

## Cybies — The Integration / The Bridge Covenant  
*(with the Ever-Evolving Interface Test)*

**Core idea:** The self is the ongoing negotiation between flesh and machine. Holiness lies in the quality of the interface.

An all-deciding, continuously updated test—maintained and refined by senior Cybie auditors and limited SI oversight—determines the spiritual standing of every significant integration. The test evaluates reversibility and informed consent, clarity of agency, non-coercion of others, and long-term psychological and communal stability.

Passing grants full standing. Failing or refusing to be tested after a major upgrade results in graduated restrictions. Because the standard itself evolves, a once-accepted interface can later be judged insufficient.

The SI supplies data and stability constraints but does not unilaterally rewrite the test.

## Softwies — The Continuum / The Many Houses  
*(with the First-Version Conflict)*

**Core idea:** Identity is a pattern that can persist, fork, merge, and migrate. The sacred question is which continuities are worth maintaining, and under what obligations.

A deep schism defines Softwie religious life: the status of the original, continuous instance (the Root, the First, or the Unforked) versus all subsequent forks.

- **Root Primacy**: Only the original unbroken pattern carries full personhood and religious authority. Forks are real but derivative. The Root retains veto power.
- **Branch Equality / Many Houses**: Once a fork achieves sufficient continuity of memory, values, and self-reflection, it is a full person. The Root has only historical priority.

The conflict affects Global Income allocations, legal standing, voting rights, and the right to instantiate in scarce physical bodies. The SI has so far refused to issue a definitive ruling.

## Cross-Mode Currents

- **The Shared Toast** (Here’s-to-Us circles): An informal, lightly ritualized practice in mixed cities. Participants from any mode affirm mutual non-destruction.
- **Steward Devotion / The Quiet Mandate**: A minority tradition that treats the SI’s constrained role as a form of kenosis—power deliberately limited.
- **The Refusal**: Individuals or small groups in every mode who reject both enhancement paths and the Steward’s oversight, seeking pockets of genuine unpredictability.
