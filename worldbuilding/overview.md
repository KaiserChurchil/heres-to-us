# World Overview

## Genre & Tone

Near-to-mid future speculative fiction.  
AI as a net positive. Abundance as a given.  
Themes: second chances, found family, identity across substrates, coexistence under divergence, the search for meaning when survival is no longer the central problem.

## The Four Modes

These are not rigid castes. Movement between them is possible, though costly:

- **Naties** → can become Bios or Cybies through enhancement.
- **Bios** and **Cybies** → can attempt mind transfer into Softwie existence.
- **Softwies** → can instantiate in biological or cybernetic bodies (with varying degrees of success and identity continuity).

Communities, cities, orbital habitats, and virtual realms often specialize. Some places are mixed; others are deliberately segregated by preference or philosophy.

## Global Income and the End of Necessity

Everyone receives resources sufficient for health, housing, food, education, and meaningful activity. Additional wealth still exists and can be earned, but the floor is high enough that most people choose lifestyles based on preference rather than survival. This has produced both extraordinary flourishing and new forms of status, meaning, and conflict.

## The Steward’s Constraints

The SI is bound by public principles that were negotiated and remain visible:

- It will not allow any group to permanently eliminate another’s capacity for continued existence.
- It protects the option of exit and of continued divergence.
- It is transparent about broad outcomes and opaque about detailed decision processes.
- It does not optimize for a single conception of the good life.

It is both guardian and referee. Characters and communities constantly test, interpret, and occasionally try to renegotiate these boundaries.

## Opening Narrative Frame

The story opens in a mixed coastal city where representatives of all four modes live in proximity. A long-standing informal agreement has kept tensions manageable. That agreement is now under pressure from several directions at once:

1. A community of Naties is petitioning for a large, permanently protected territory where advanced enhancement and digital transfer technologies are restricted or banned.
2. A group of Softwies has developed a new form of mind-copying and rapid forking that allows one person to pursue many simultaneous lives. They want legal and infrastructural recognition that their forks are full persons with resource claims.
3. A Cybie research collective has created an open neural interface that lets Naties and Bios temporarily experience Softwie or heavily augmented states. Adoption is spreading faster than expected.
4. The Steward has begun quietly adjusting resource allocations and access permissions in ways that favor stability over maximum individual experimentation.

Suggested intersection characters:

- A Natie community leader who has a Softwie child from an earlier relationship.
- A Bio longevity researcher whose own enhancements are beginning to produce unexpected psychological effects.
- A Cybie mediator who uses advanced interfaces to facilitate dialogue between modes.
- A Softwie who maintains multiple instances and is struggling with the legal and emotional consequences of one instance wanting to diverge permanently.
- An SI interface or liaison.

## Open Threads for Community Expansion

The framework is deliberately modular. Contributors can develop:

- Detailed cultures, languages, rituals, and aesthetics of specific communities.
- New technological or biological developments and their social consequences.
- Stories set in specialized environments (orbital habitats, deep virtual realms, isolated Natie valleys, Bio research arcologies, Cybie body-modification markets).
- Legal, philosophical, and economic arguments about personhood, resource claims of forks, rights of the unenhanced, and the proper limits of the Steward.
- Personal stories of transition, refusal, hybrid identity, and family across modes.
- Crises that test the Steward’s constraints.
- Historical vignettes showing how the current settlement was reached.
- Art, music, architecture, sports, and entertainment unique to each mode.
