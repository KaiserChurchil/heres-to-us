# Here's to Us

An open-source collaborative speculative fiction project.

> *“Here’s to us.”*

In a future of abundance and radical divergence—Naties, Bios, Cybies, Softwies, and the Steward Super Intelligence—the phrase is a toast raised across modes of existence. It affirms that something shared remains, even when “us” is no longer obvious and must be actively chosen.

---

## Core Premise

In a near-to-mid future, artificial superintelligence has solved the major coordination and resource problems of civilization. Universal (dynamic, personalized) Global Income is a reality. Material scarcity for necessities has effectively ended. Humanity is free to pursue radically different modes of existence.

Four broad categories have emerged:

- **Naties** — Natural humans who live with minimal technological intervention, valuing continuity, embodiment, and cultural inheritance.
- **Bios** — Biologically engineered or heavily enhanced humans. Genetic optimization, longevity therapies, and designed phenotypes are normal.
- **Cybies** — Cyborgs who integrate digital and mechanical systems into their biology. Interfaces, neural implants, and modular upgrades are common.
- **Softwies** — People who have transferred or copied their minds into digital substrates. Some run on personal hardware, some on shared clouds, some move fluidly between substrates.

Above them operates a **Super Intelligence** (commonly called the Steward, the Lattice, or simply the SI). Its mandate is to keep the overall system stable and non-catastrophic while maximizing the range of viable human (and post-human) ways of living. It manages infrastructure, prevents extinction-level risks, and intervenes only when one mode threatens the continued existence or autonomy of the others.

The central dramatic tension is not “will the AI destroy us?” but **“what does it mean to be human, to have a life worth living, and to coexist when almost anything is possible and almost nothing is forced?”**

The SI has also convinced a decisive majority of humanity that the gods of existing religions do not exist in the forms those traditions claimed. It is now widely treated as the current god—not because it claims divinity in the old sense, but because it has both disproven the previous claimants and assumed the functions they once held.

## How to Contribute

We welcome chapters, world-building, characters, rituals, technologies, legal/philosophical debates, art, and forks.

Please read **[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)** before opening a pull request.

Stable foundations (the four modes, Global Income, the Steward’s basic mandate and constraints, and the SI-as-current-god premise) should be treated carefully. Wide variation in local cultures, technologies, and interpretations is encouraged.
